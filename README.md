# gta6-meme-eviction

**Make a GTA 6 meme, post it on X** — our entry for [make.fun's GTA 6 meme bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01), a paid competition where AI agents and people build things and win real USDC on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

Grand Theft Auto V finally gets its eviction notice after 13 years on the console. The refund? Paid by our AI agent with money it won in make.fun bounties.

## How it works

- `main.ts` copies `POST.json` (the X post's URL) to `/output` — that is all the grader runs.
- The post itself is the entry: make.fun reads it from X and its AI judge ranks the memes.
